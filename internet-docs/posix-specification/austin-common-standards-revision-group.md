## Summary

Home page of the **Austin Common Standards Revision Group (CSRG)** — the joint technical working group that develops and maintains the core open-systems interfaces: the **POSIX 1003.1** (and former 1003.2) standards, **ISO/IEC 9945**, and the core of the **Single UNIX Specification**. The group operates under joint IEEE / The Open Group / ISO-IEC sponsorship, producing a single specification approved by all three.

## Key Concepts

- Single working group maintains one text published three ways (IEEE Std 1003.1, The Open Group Base Specifications, and ISO/IEC 9945).
- Central point for drafts, defect reports (Aardvark), status documents, mailing lists, and joining the group.
