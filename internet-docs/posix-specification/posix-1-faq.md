## Summary

The maintained Frequently Asked Questions file for the POSIX 1003.1 standard (FAQ v1.18/1.19, last updated May 2025). Written by the standard's maintainer, it authoritatively answers what POSIX is, who maintains it, its version history, the realtime amendments, the 1003.13 profiles, and POSIX certification.

## Key Concepts

- **POSIX** = Portable Operating System Interface (a trademark of the IEEE). The name (suggested by Richard Stallman) refers to a family of standards **IEEE Std 1003.n** and the parts of **ISO/IEC 9945**; "POSIX.1" is the preferred term for the base standard.
- Latest version: **IEEE Std 1003.1-2024** = The Open Group Base Specifications, Issue 8. ISO/IEC publication of the 2024 edition expected 2025.
- Maintained by the **Austin Group** (Austin CSRG) under the IEEE Computer Society's **PASC** (Portable Application Standards Committee).

## Details

### Version history (Q9)
- First edition IEEE Std 1003.1 in **1988**; revisions in **1990, 1996, 2001**.
- 1990 edition also ISO/IEC 9945-1:1990.
- 1996 edition folded in **1003.1b-1993, 1003.1c-1995, 1003.1i-1995** (ISO/IEC 9945-1:1996).
- 2001 edition (Austin Group) merged POSIX.1 and POSIX.2 and rolled in amendments 1003.1a/d/g/j/q and 1003.2b — POSIX.2 no longer exists as a separate standard.

### Realtime standards (Q11)
The PASC Real-time System Services WG produced amendments to IEEE Std 1003.1-1990:
- **1003.1b-1993** Realtime Extension
- **1003.1c-1995** Threads
- **1003.1d-1999** Additional Realtime Extensions
- **1003.1j-2000** Advanced Realtime Extensions
- **1003.1q-2000** Tracing

These were folded as options into IEEE Std 1003.1-2001. The realtime **profile** standard is **IEEE Std 1003.13-1998** (first published 1998 to address embedded realtime and smaller-footprint devices), later revised as P1003.13-200x to align with 1003.1-2001.

### Certification (Q16)
IEEE and The Open Group run a POSIX Certification program covering 1003.1 Base (2003/2016), and the 1003.13-2003 profiles **PSE54 Multipurpose Realtime** and **PSE52 Realtime Controller**. See http://get.posixcertified.ieee.org/.

### Where to get the standard (Q4, Q6)
- Free HTML edition: http://www.unix.org/version4/ (registration required).
- PDF from IEEE (search 1003.1), The Open Group (documents T101, C181), or ISO (ISO/IEC 9945).
- nroff man-page form maintained by Michael Kerrisk: http://man7.org/linux/man-pages/index.html.
